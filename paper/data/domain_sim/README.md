# Simulated Spatial Domains

This three-modality simulation comes from the
[SpatialGlue triplet-omics tutorial](https://spatialglue-tutorials.readthedocs.io/en/latest/Tutorial%205_data%20integration%20for%20triplet%20omics%20data%20%28Simulation%29.html).
Its RNA, ADT, and ATAC measurements represent the same 1,296 spatial
observations.

| Modality | Features |
|---|---:|
| RNA | 1,000 |
| ADT | 100 |
| ATAC | 1,000 |

The three modalities share aligned two-dimensional spatial coordinates and
simulation factors. Four spatial factors plus a background state define five
ground-truth spatial classes. Three additional nonspatial factors are recorded.
These are simulated measurements rather than observations from tissue.
