# Axolotl Telencephalon Stereo-seq

Development and regeneration spatial transcriptomics data come from
[CNGB STDS0000056](https://db.cngb.org/stomics/datasets/STDS0000056).
The source contains spatial RNA measurements of axolotl telencephalon across
six developmental samples (stages 44, 54, 57, juvenile, adult, and
metamorphosed) and eight regeneration samples (control and 2, 5, 10, 15, 20,
30, and 60 days after injury). These are stage or time-point samples, not
independent biological replicates of one condition.
